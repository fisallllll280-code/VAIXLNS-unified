Operating sequence

From sources to verified work

01 · Discover

Scan repository documents, code, policies, and tests. Attach stable IDs and content digests.

02 · Import sessions

Review and place approved exports under sessions/. Keep private exports out of public hosting.

03 · Index and search

Generate var/knowledge/index.json and search by terms. Add semantic retrieval only after evaluation.

04 · Assign and verify

Use role contracts to plan work; require evidence, tests, and explicit approval for mutations.
